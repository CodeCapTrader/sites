Your Privacy Matters

LoanPrep ("the App", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application.

Key Principles

  • Your loan assessment inputs and related financial information are intended to remain on your device
  • We do not operate an account system or our own cloud database for your loan assessment inputs
  • Transparent about advertising and data collection
  • Controls for locally stored app content and applicable advertising choices
  • Privacy practices are applied subject to applicable law
Important

LoanPrep is primarily a local tool application. Your personal financial information is stored locally on your device. However, the App includes advertising features and notification services as described in this Policy.

Information We Collect

LoanPrep is designed to operate locally on your device. We collect and process only the information necessary to provide our services:

Assessment Information (Stored Locally)

  • Selected app region, loan amount and loan purpose
  • Monthly income range and employment status
  • Credit history category
  • Whether you report having valid identification and the type of bank account you select
  • Age range, which is stored for display and is not used in the current eligibility calculation
  • Asset-ownership categories you select
  • For assessments created through an earlier assessment flow, employment duration, existing debts, and collateral type or value, if provided

App Usage Data (Stored Locally)

  • Points balance and transaction history
  • Daily check-in streaks
  • Rewarded video watch history
  • Checklist items and progress
  • Assessment results and history
  • Reports you choose to save, including a custom report name and notes you enter
  • Purchase and points-redemption records used to calculate remaining report credits and entitlement status. A local record is automatically removed after all report credits represented by that record have been used
  • App preferences and state, including appearance, currency, selected region, reminder settings and times, onboarding state, your Usage Analytics choice, and a manually selected legal-document language
  • A limited queue of analytics events awaiting retry if Firebase is not yet available. Nothing is added to this queue while Usage Analytics is off, and the queue is removed by Delete All Data

Advertising Frequency and Timing Data (Local)

So that we can limit how often advertisements appear and reduce the risk of showing them at inappropriate moments, the App uses the following local information:

  • Persistent on-device counters and dates, including the number of full-screen advertisements shown for applicable daily limits, the time the most recent one was shown, installation date, recent active days and app-open counts
  • A simple activity tier that your device derives from those counters — for example, whether you are a new, occasional or frequent user of the App. It is recalculated on your device and is used only to decide how many full-screen advertisements may be shown and how far apart they must be
  • Temporary in-memory session signals, including the current-session advertisement count, number of relevant screen transitions, time spent on an eligible screen and recent rapid back-navigation timing. These signals reset when the applicable session or process resets
  • The timestamp of the most recent observed screen tap, held temporarily in memory and used to abort a pending full-screen advertisement when a tap has just occurred. The App does not record what you tapped, where you tapped or the content of the interaction

These App-managed advertising-frequency and timing signals, together with the activity tier derived from them, remain local. They are not transmitted off your device, are not uploaded to our own servers, and are not used to select or personalize the advertisements you see. This local pacing is separate from advertising interaction and measurement data processed by Google or an advertising provider after an ad request or impression.

Advertising Consent Information

Where an advertising consent form is required, your choices are managed by Google's User Messaging Platform and stored or otherwise processed by Google and on your device in accordance with Google's consent framework and privacy documentation.

Purchase Information

  • Product purchased (single report or package)
  • Number of reports included and used
  • Apple transaction ID and product ID
  • Purchase date

Advertising Data (Collected by Third Parties)

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only with your consent
  • Device and app information (device type, OS version, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, video completions, ad format and placement)
  • Ad performance and revenue measurement data (the ad network that served an ad and the estimated revenue of an impression)
  • Coarse location (country/region level)

Where required by law, the App does not release an advertising request until Google's User Messaging Platform reports that an ad request may be made. That status can reflect consent, a choice to receive limited or non-personalized advertising, or a determination that a consent form is not required. The data used for a particular request depends on your choices, device settings, applicable law, Google's configuration and the participating advertising provider.

Analytics Data (Collected by Firebase)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and measure purchases and advertising performance. Depending on SDK behavior, device settings and our event configuration, Firebase may collect or receive:

  • Device and app information (model, OS version, screen size, app version, app-instance and other permitted device identifiers)
  • App usage data (screens viewed, session duration, app opens, feature interactions)
  • Purchase events (transaction amount, currency, product ID, Apple transaction ID and quantity — no payment card information)
  • In-app events (points earned or spent, points balance, rewarded video completions and daily rewarded-video count). A points redemption may include an app-generated transaction reference used to prevent duplicate event reporting; it is not an Apple payment transaction ID
  • Advertising performance events (ad impressions, ad format, ad placement, the ad network that served the ad, and the estimated revenue of an impression)
  • Approximate location (country/region level based on IP address)
  • Technical and app-lifecycle information made available by the Firebase Analytics SDK

Firebase Analytics collection is switched off when the App is installed. The App keeps collection off until Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow has completed, and in regions where a consent form is required it additionally waits until Google reports that requests may be made. The resulting choice is remembered for later launches. You can turn Firebase Analytics collection off at any time via Profile → Data Management → Usage Analytics; while it is off, the App neither sends analytics events nor stores them on your device for later retry. UMP controls advertising consent and whether the App releases advertising requests; ATT controls access to the IDFA for tracking.

Important Clarifications
  • Firebase does NOT receive your personal financial information (income, debts, loan details) — this data remains stored on your device only
  • The App does not send your saved-report name, saved-report notes, name, email address or phone number to Firebase. Do not put information in a saved-report name or note that you do not want stored locally or included in a report you choose to share
  • Firebase Analytics data is pseudonymous: it is keyed to an app-instance identifier rather than to your name or contact details
  • We do not send your financial inputs to Firebase or use those inputs to create advertising audiences
  • Because Google provides both our analytics and advertising services, advertising and conversion events may be processed across AdMob, Firebase and connected Google services for measurement, attribution, reporting and optimization, subject to our service settings, your applicable choices and Google's privacy policy
  • Where required, advertising requests and certain advertising-related processing are subject to your choices in Google's consent form. Firebase Analytics may process technical and usage data separately from AdMob advertising requests, as described in this Policy and subject to applicable law

Notification Information (Stored Locally)

If you grant notification permission, the App uses Apple's local notification system to schedule optional daily check-in and weekly checklist reminders on your device. We do not register the App for remote push notifications or send a push-notification device token to our servers.

What We DON'T Collect
  • We do not provide account-registration or dedicated fields requesting your name, email address, phone number or street address. Information you voluntarily type into a saved-report name or note remains locally stored unless you choose to share the report
  • Precise GPS location data
  • Photos or media from your device
  • Browsing history outside the App

System Permissions We Do Not Request

LoanPrep's own feature code does not request access to your contacts, photo library, camera, microphone, precise location, health data or Bluetooth, and does not perform local-network discovery or scanning. The only permissions the App may request are notification permission for local reminders and App Tracking Transparency permission for advertising, as described in this Policy. The App declares no other permission purpose strings. Any network behaviour of a third-party SDK is subject to that SDK's version, configuration and provider documentation.

How We Use Your Information

Your loan assessment inputs and core assessment processing remain local to your device. Other technical, purchase, analytics and advertising data may be processed by Apple or Google as described in this Policy. We use information to:

Provide Core Features

  • Generate loan preparation assessment reports using automated analysis
  • Create preparation checklists based on your inputs
  • Track your progress and activities
  • Manage points and daily check-in features
  • Process rewarded video completions for points
  • Store your preferences and settings

Fulfill Purchases

  • Track available report credits
  • Maintain the local purchase or redemption records currently needed to calculate unused report credits; records with no remaining credits are removed during local cleanup
  • Verify entitlements for paid features

Display Advertisements

  • Show banner advertisements, including collapsible banner formats, within the App
  • Show native advertisements that are styled to match the surrounding content and are always labelled as advertising
  • Display rewarded video advertisements (optional and user-initiated)
  • Display interstitial (full-screen) advertisements at natural break points, such as when you leave a checklist detail page or close a history panel
  • Show app open advertisements on launch or when returning to the App

Control How Often Advertisements Appear

  • Limit the number of full-screen advertisements shown per session and per day
  • Adjust those limits according to a simple activity tier that your device derives from how long the App has been installed and how often you have opened it recently, so that newer or occasional users see fewer full-screen advertisements
  • Keep advertisements away from sensitive moments, such as while a purchase is being completed or while your assessment report is being generated and first delivered
  • Avoid displaying a full-screen advertisement immediately after you tap the screen, to reduce accidental clicks
  • Note: this processing happens entirely on your device. The activity tier and the counters behind it are not transmitted off your device, are not used to select or target the advertisements you see, and are not used to build an advertising or marketing profile of you

Analyze App Usage (via Firebase Analytics)

  • Understand app opens, sessions and app-lifecycle or usage trends made available by Firebase Analytics
  • Understand which features users find helpful
  • Investigate product issues reflected in the analytics events described in this Policy
  • Improve user experience based on aggregated usage patterns
  • Measure business metrics (user retention, feature adoption)
  • Note: This analysis primarily uses aggregated and pseudonymous data and is not associated by us with your name or contact information
  • The current App does not integrate Firebase Crashlytics or Firebase Performance Monitoring and does not intentionally send crash reports, stack traces or custom performance traces through those products
  • Note: this analysis only takes place while Usage Analytics is on. It is off until the advertising consent flow has completed and can be switched off at any time in Profile → Data Management
Important

We do NOT use your personal financial information for:

  • Advertising or marketing profiling
  • Sharing with third parties
  • Cross-app tracking of personal data
  • Building personal profiles on external servers

Data Storage & Security

Your loan assessment inputs, assessment results, checklists and related financial information are stored within the App's local iOS sandbox using local persistence mechanisms such as UserDefaults and local files. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, your iOS device may automatically backup LoanPrep data as part of your device backup. You can manage this in your iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Your Responsibility

Advertising & Tracking

LoanPrep includes advertising features powered by Google AdMob.

Types of Advertisements

  • Banner Ads: Displayed in designated areas of a screen. Some banners may first appear in an expanded (collapsible) form that you can collapse back to a standard banner.
  • Native Ads: Advertisements styled to match the surrounding content. They are always labelled as advertising and are clearly distinguishable from our own content.
  • Rewarded Video Ads: Entirely optional videos that you choose to watch in exchange for in-app points.
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen advertisements shown only at natural break points — for example when you leave a checklist detail page, close a history panel, or exit a previously saved report. They are never shown while the App is starting up, while a purchase is in progress, while content is loading, or while your assessment report is being generated and first delivered to you.
  • App Open Ads: May appear when the App is launched or when you return to it from the background. They are never shown on your first launch after installing the App.

Where required by law, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to determine whether a consent form must be presented and whether advertising may be requested. How We Limit Advertising

To keep advertising from becoming intrusive, the App applies limits that are calculated entirely on your device:

  • Daily and per-session limits on the number of full-screen advertisements
  • A minimum interval between two full-screen advertisements, which widens the more advertisements you have already seen in a session
  • No full-screen advertisement immediately after you tap the screen, to reduce accidental clicks
  • No advertising during payment, during report generation, or on core report delivery screens

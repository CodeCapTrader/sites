1. Summary (plain-language overview)

  • The App is designed to work on your device. The vehicle details, preferences, and estimates you create are stored locally on your device, not on our servers.
  • We do not operate servers that receive your content, and we do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs in the current version. The App's only external connections are to Apple for optional in-app purchases, purchase restoration, and app-rating prompts.
  • We do not require an account, and we do not ask you to provide your name, email, address, or government identifiers to use the App.
  • We do not access your GPS or precise location. A default region/state is inferred only from your device's locale and time-zone settings and is used on-device.
  • Payments for optional in-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never receive your full payment-card details.
  • The current version of the App does not display third-party advertisements and does not use advertising identifiers. Future versions may include advertising (see Section 8).
  • We do not sell or "share" your personal information as those terms are defined under applicable U.S. state privacy laws.

This summary is for convenience only; please read the full policy below.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the LoanGauge mobile application and any related services that link to it. It does not apply to third-party services, websites, or products that we do not control, including Apple's App Store, Google's services, or any lender you may contact.

3. Information we collect

We practice data minimization and collect as little information as possible.

a. Information you provide and create in the App.

When you use the App you may enter vehicle information (such as category, make, model, year, mileage, condition, title status, outstanding balance, and a desired amount), select a state/region, and use checklists. This information, along with the estimates it generates and your preferences (such as appearance mode and any unlocked content), is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit this content to our servers, and we cannot access it. If you export a report as a PDF, the file is generated on your device; it leaves your device only if and where you choose to share it (for example, through the iOS share sheet).

b. Purchase information.

If you make an optional in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store using your Apple ID. Apple may share with us limited, non–payment-card information such as a transaction identifier and confirmation of purchase or restoration, so we can unlock the content you bought. We do not receive your credit- or debit-card number.

c. Device and technical information.

The App may process basic technical information on your device (such as device model, operating-system version, language, region/time-zone settings, and app version) to operate correctly, format currency and content, and infer a default region. This processing occurs on-device.

d. Diagnostics you choose to share via Apple.

If you have enabled sharing of diagnostics and usage data in your iOS settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymized crash and usage reports through App Analytics. This data is controlled by Apple and does not identify you to us. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

e. App ratings.

If you choose to rate the App, the rating prompt is presented by Apple. Your review and rating are handled by Apple under Apple's terms; we do not receive personal information from this feature.

f. Advertising identifiers. Ad versions only

Applies only to versions that display ads — see Section 8.

The current version does not collect advertising identifiers. In future versions that include advertising, our advertising provider (Google AdMob) may collect device and advertising identifiers as described in Section 8.

4. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, or government-issued identifiers.
  • We do not access your precise or GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles on our servers.

5. How we use information

We use the limited information described above to:

  • provide and operate the App's educational features and generate on-device estimates;
  • localize content, currency, and default region;
  • process and restore your optional in-app purchases (via Apple);
  • maintain the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the App;
  • diagnose problems and improve the App using aggregated, anonymized information; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you. Estimates are illustrative and are not lending decisions.

6. Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK users)

Where the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the App and process purchases you request.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the App secure, functional, and improved, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent — where required, for example before serving personalized advertising or non-essential tracking in future ad-supported versions. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable law.

7. How your information is stored and retained

  • Local storage. Content you create in the App is stored on your device using the operating system's standard app-storage mechanisms. It remains until you delete it (for example, using the App's "Reset All Data" function or by uninstalling the App).
  • Retention. Because we do not store your App content on our servers, we do not retain it. Purchase records maintained by Apple are subject to Apple's retention practices. Any aggregated, anonymized diagnostics are retained only as long as needed for the purposes described.

Consistent with this design, our Apple App Store privacy disclosures (the App Store “privacy label”) reflect that the current version does not collect personal data through the App or link it to your identity. Where the label reflects data such as purchase activity, that processing is performed by Apple to deliver the in-app purchases you request.

8. Advertising AdMob

Current status.

The current version of the App does not display third-party advertisements, does not use an advertising SDK, and does not collect advertising identifiers for ad purposes.

Future ad-supported versions.

A future version of the App may display advertisements served through Google AdMob. If and when advertising is introduced:

  • AdMob and its partners may collect and process device information and identifiers (such as the device advertising identifier / IDFA, IP address, and general ad-interaction data) to deliver and measure ads and to limit repetitive ads.
  • On iOS, we will present Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt where required. If you do not grant permission, ads will be limited to non-personalized advertising.
  • You can control ad personalization through your device settings (for example, iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and → Apple Advertising) and through Google's ad settings.
  • Google's handling of data in this context is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and its advertising terms. See policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

When advertising is enabled, we will update this policy and its effective date accordingly.

9. Analytics

The current version does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs. We may use Apple's aggregated, anonymized App Analytics as described in Section 3(d). If a future version introduces third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools, we will describe them here and update the effective date.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding personal information about you: to access, correct, delete, port, restrict, or object to processing, and to withdraw consent.

  • Because most of your App content is stored only on your device, you can exercise deletion directly at any time using "Reset All Data" in the App or by uninstalling it.
  • EEA/UK (GDPR): you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): you have rights to know, delete, and correct, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising. In the current version, we do not sell or share personal information as defined by these laws. If a future ad-supported version involves "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising, it will occur only with your consent through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and you will be able to opt out at any time via your device settings (see Section 8). We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
  • Other U.S. states and other jurisdictions: you may have comparable rights under applicable local law.

To make a request that involves information we control, contact us at yumiao.dev@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request. For information held by Apple or Google, please use their respective privacy controls.

13. Children's privacy

Because any information entered in the App is stored only on the device and is not transmitted to or accessible by us, a parent or guardian can remove all of that content directly at any time using “Reset All Data” in the App or by uninstalling it. If a child has provided personal information to us directly through a channel we control (for example, by emailing us), contact us at yumiao.dev@gmail.com and we will delete the information we hold. We comply with applicable children's-privacy laws, including COPPA (U.S.) and the GDPR's provisions on children where relevant.
